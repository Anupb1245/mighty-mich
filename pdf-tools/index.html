Why local, not cloud?

Most online PDF sites upload your file, run it on their servers and promise to delete it in an hour. Contracts, medical records, pay slips: that is a lot of trust for a merge.

Inkfold does the work with the PDF engine already inside your browser. Your file stays in your tab's memory and is gone when you close it.

  • No upload. Works offline once the page is loaded.
  • No account, no limit. Not two tasks a day. As many as you like.
  • No subscription. Nothing to cancel, nothing to forget.
  • No tracking. No cookies, no analytics, no third-party scripts.
  • Open source. MIT licensed. Host it on your own domain.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier, because there is no server to pay for. The work happens on your device.

How big a file can I use?

There is no hard limit. Files over a couple of hundred megabytes can be slow because everything happens in the browser's memory.

What about Word, Excel and PowerPoint?

Converting Office files properly needs an Office engine, which does not fit in a browser. Inkfold sticks to what it can do well locally: everything PDF, plus images.

Does compression keep my text selectable?

Yes, for the Recommended and Strong levels: they shrink the images inside the PDF and leave the text alone. Only the Maximum level turns pages into pictures.

Can it open a PDF I lost the password for?

No. Unlock removes a password you know. It cannot crack one.
